Background

What This MAS Tool Does

This tool helps you adapt to anything — a tool, an environment, a skill, or a social situation. It works like a personal coach in three simple steps:

  1. Describe: Name your target and write down what you notice — what you see, feel, hear, what keeps going wrong, and what you tried.
  2. Plan: One click turns your observations into a step-by-step adaptation roadmap.
  3. Evolve: After each practice run, log your measured result. The system tracks your trend, updates your adaptation phase (from First Hit to Immunity), and tells you the exact next move.
  4. Deep dive (advanced): Optionally run 9 AI agents — each with a different thinking personality — and merge them into one synthesis.

Steps 1-3 run locally, no external AI required. Step 4 uses Google Gemini when the server has an API key. Save a plan to Template Memory to reuse it later.
