Background

What This MAS Tool Does

This tool helps you adapt to anything — a tool, an environment, a skill, or a social situation. It works like a personal coach:

  1. Name your target: What do you want to get better at? Be specific.
  2. Write down what you notice: What do you see, feel, hear? What went wrong? What did you try?
  3. Get a plan: The tool turns your observations into a step-by-step adaptation plan.
  4. Follow the plan: Do the drills, track your results, and repeat.
  5. Save or share: Copy the plan for later, or save it as a reusable template.

No external AI or internet connection is required. The plan is generated locally from what you type. The more detail you give, the better the plan.
