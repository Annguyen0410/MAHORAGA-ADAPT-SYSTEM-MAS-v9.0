##  WHO IS MAHORAGA?

**Name:** Eight-Handled Sword Divergent Sila Divine General Mahoraga  
**(八握剣異戒脱将・摩虎羅 - Yatsuka-no-Tsurugi Ikai Dashō: Makora)**

**Origin:** *Jujutsu Kaisen* (呪術廻戦) by Gege Akutami.  
**Classification:** The most powerful Shikigami of the **Ten Shadows Technique** (used by the Zenin Clan).

Mahoraga is a mythological beast, a "Divine General" that no Ten Shadows user in history (until Sukuna) was ever able to tame. It represents the ultimate apex of survival and evolution.

---

##  THE CORE POWER: INFINITE ADAPTATION

Mahoraga's ability is simple but absolute: **It can adapt to any and all phenomena.**

### 1. The Eight-Handled Wheel (The Dharmachakra)
On its head sits a golden wheel with eight handles. This wheel is the engine of its evolution.
- **The Spin:** When Mahoraga is hit by an attack or encounters a phenomenon, the wheel spins.
- **The Process:** Once the wheel completes a turn, Mahoraga becomes "immune" or "adapted" to that specific event.
- **The Outcome:** It doesn't just block an attack; it evolves its own nature to nullify the threat and find a counter-measure.

### 2. Adaptation Types
- **Defensive Adaptation:** Neutralizing damage from an element (e.g., fire, cutting, gravity).
- **Offensive Adaptation:** Evolving its attacks to bypass the target's defenses (e.g., cutting through "Infinity").
- **Constitutional Adaptation:** Changing its own biological or cursed energy makeup to survive a hostile environment.

---

## ⚔️ EQUIPMENT: THE SWORD OF EXTERMINATION

Mahoraga wields a massive blade attached to its right arm, coated in **Positive Energy**.
- This blade is lethal to Cursed Spirits (who are made of negative energy).
- Through adaptation, the blade can also change its properties to cut through any physical or metaphysical barrier.
