## II. THE 7 LAYERS OF OPERATION

MAS v8.5 functions through seven distinct but interconnected layers of "Predictive Embodied Meta-Learning."

### 1. Layer 1: Raw Perception (Sensorium)
The collection of high-fidelity sensory data. The Human is the primary sensor (touch, pressure, micro-vibrations).

### 2. Layer 2: Statistical Modeling (Inference)
The AI analyzes the data to find the most probable patterns. This is the "Engine" of the wheel.

### 3. Layer 3: Prediction & Testing (The Hypothesis)
The system doesn't just react; it predicts. "If I move my finger 2mm left, the pencil lead will flex by X amount."

### 4. Layer 4: Error Computation (The Delta)
The most critical part. The system calculates the distance between the **Prediction** and the **Outcome**. This error is the "Food" for the wheel.

### 5. Layer 5: Recursive Refinement (The Spin)
The model is updated instantly. The next prediction is more accurate than the last. This is the "Turn of the Wheel."

### 6. Layer 6: Invariant Extraction (Abstraction)
The system identifies what *never changes* across different tests. This is the **Essence**.

### 7. Layer 7: Embodied Mastery (Assimilation)
The knowledge is pushed down into the motor cortex and reflexes. Conscious thought is no longer required.
